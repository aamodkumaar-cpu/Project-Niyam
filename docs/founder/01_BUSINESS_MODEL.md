# Project Niyam — Business Model

> **Our goal is to become the trusted AI operating platform for Indian businesses, beginning with compliance.**

---

# Problem

Small and medium businesses in India face a growing compliance burden.

They must regularly understand and comply with regulations related to:

* GST
* Income Tax
* TDS
* PF
* ESIC
* Labour Laws
* Shops & Establishment
* MCA filings
* Professional Tax
* MSME schemes
* State-specific regulations

Most businesses depend on accountants, consultants, or legal professionals even for routine questions because reliable information is scattered across government portals, circulars, notifications, and legal documents.

The result is unnecessary cost, delays, and uncertainty.

---

# Our Solution

Project Niyam provides a specialized AI assistant that retrieves trusted regulatory information, explains it in plain language, cites authoritative sources, and progressively automates compliance workflows.

Instead of searching across multiple websites and PDFs, businesses interact with a single AI assistant designed specifically for compliance.
