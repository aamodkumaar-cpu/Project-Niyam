# Project Niyam — Founder's Manifesto

> **Our mission is to make regulatory and operational knowledge instantly accessible, trustworthy, and actionable for every Indian business.**

---

# Why Project Niyam Exists

Running a business should be about creating value—not struggling to interpret regulations, search through government notifications, or repeatedly consult multiple experts for routine compliance questions.

Every year, businesses spend countless hours understanding changing rules related to taxation, labour laws, statutory filings, registrations, and operational compliance.

Most small and medium businesses cannot afford dedicated legal or compliance teams. As regulations evolve, they rely on fragmented information spread across PDFs, government websites, consultants, and messaging groups.

The information exists.

Finding the correct answer at the right time remains the challenge.

Project Niyam exists to solve this problem.

---

# Our Vision

We envision a future where every business has access to an intelligent AI assistant that understands regulations, retrieves trustworthy information, explains it in plain language, and helps users complete compliance tasks with confidence.

The assistant should not merely answer questions.

It should become a trusted operational partner.

---

# Our Long-Term Goal

Project Niyam is not a chatbot.

Project Niyam is an AI Platform.

The platform will power specialized AI assistants for multiple business domains, beginning with compliance and expanding into finance, HR, legal, procurement, operations, and other enterprise functions.

---

# Our First Product

The first product built on Project Niyam will be:

**AI Compliance Officer for Indian Businesses**

This product will help businesses:

* understand regulatory obligations
* answer compliance questions
* retrieve supporting documents
* explain government notifications
* identify upcoming deadlines
* recommend required registrations
* automate repetitive compliance workflows

---

# Our Engineering Principles

Every engineering decision should follow these principles:

## Trust before intelligence

Answers must be grounded in verifiable sources.

Users should understand where an answer came from.

---

## Platform before product

Reusable platform capabilities should always be preferred over one-off implementations.

Every new feature should strengthen the platform.

---

## Simplicity wins

Simple, maintainable designs outperform clever but fragile solutions.

Readable code is a long-term investment.

---

## Production quality from day one

Testing, documentation, diagnostics, versioning, and architecture are not optional activities.

Quality is built continuously.

---

## AI should assist, not replace human judgment

Project Niyam helps users make better decisions.

Final responsibility remains with the business owner or authorised professional.

---

# Technology Principles

Project Niyam will evolve incrementally.

The platform will gradually incorporate:

* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Hybrid Retrieval
* Metadata Filtering
* Re-ranking
* Long-term Memory
* AI Agents
* Multi-Agent Workflows
* MCP Integration
* Evaluation Pipelines
* Enterprise APIs
* Security
* Observability

Each capability will be added only when it provides measurable value.

---

# Success

We will measure success by the confidence users place in the platform.

The highest compliment is not that the AI sounds intelligent.

It is that businesses trust it enough to rely on it for critical operational decisions.

---

# Our Commitment

We are building Project Niyam with patience.

We value long-term architecture over shortcuts.

We believe trustworthy AI systems will outlast impressive demonstrations.

Every release should make the platform simpler, stronger, and more useful than the one before.

This is the standard we will hold ourselves to.
